# Vital (vital.io) – AI‑Powered Patient Experience Platform

> No-download, AI-powered patient engagement across hospital settings.
Supports real-time communication and service automation in Emergency, Inpatient, Access, Care Team, and Urgent Care. Deployed in 100+ hospitals, serving over 5 million patients annually.

## Company Values & Approach

### Core Principles:
  - Create beautiful, intelligent software for patients and providers  
  - Enable clear, calm, personalized guidance during health events  
  - Translate medical complexity into understandable human terms  
  - Motivate decisions with data and achievable actions  
  - Focus on the entire care continuum

### Differentiation:
  - Consumer-grade user experience without app downloads  
  - Highest patient utilization rates in the industry  
  - Minimal staff training and setup requirements  
  - Proven ROI with measurable clinical and financial outcomes  
  - Integration flexibility with existing healthcare technology stacks

## Products

- [Vital Emergency](https://vital.io/products/emergency-department-patient-experience-technology): Guides ER patients with wait-time predictions, plain‑language test/exam explanations, and discharge checklists. 60%+ adoption; significant reductions in LWOBS/AMA and LOS; 10–15% NPS uplift.

- [Vital Inpatient](https://vital.io/products/inpatient-patient-experience-technology): Enables in-hospital care goals, meal orders, and live “Care Summary” updates. Over 50% usage; ~13 hr LOS reduction; 6% fewer 30‑day readmissions; saves 2,200 staff hours annually.

- [Vital Access](https://vital.io/products/vital-access): Automates post-discharge follow-ups by intelligently matching patients to providers by diagnosis, insurance, location, and language. Delivers ~30% appointment completion (industry norm: 3–4%).

- [Vital Care Team](https://vital.io/products/vital-care-team): Routes non‑clinical requests (e.g., food, charger, lactation consult) to the right staff via secure OAuth2/SSO and PHI-free workflows, saving thousands of coordination hours.

- [Vital Urgent Care](https://vital.io/products/urgent-care-patient-experience-technology): Improves walk-in clinics through check-in support, wait-time updates, result summaries, and discharge guidance. 60%+ adoption; +$500K follow-up revenue per site; +1.3 Google stars.
